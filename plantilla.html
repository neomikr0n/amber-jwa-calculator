Fusion tree — top to bottom

It is read downwards: you start at the chosen creature and walk down through every ingredient needed to create it, in cascade. The DNA figures are what has to be gathered, already discounting what you have saved.
Every creature in the tree is editable: type its level and the DNA you have, or untick Created if it does not exist yet. It is saved immediately and recalculates everything hanging from it.
With the box ticked you get what actually has to be gathered. If you untick it, the calculation imitates paleo.gg, which assumes the ingredients «are already ready to fuse» and only counts the DNA consumed in the fusions.
